Rías Baixas wines delivered in the UK

On Spain's green Atlantic coast, Rías Baixas is the home of Albariño: fresh, aromatic whites with citrus, stone fruit and a saline edge — a natural match for seafood.

We have 7 Rías Baixas wines (white) from £11.20 to £79.55 a bottle, from Galicia. Mix them with any other wine in our shop.

Prices are per bottle when you buy 6 of the same wine (1–2 bottles +5%, 3–5 bottles +3%). All prices include UK duty and VAT. Free UK delivery on orders of £150 or more; otherwise £10.80 per box of 6.
