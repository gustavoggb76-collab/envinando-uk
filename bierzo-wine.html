Bierzo wines delivered in the UK

A green, mountainous corner of north-west León where the Mencía grape gives fragrant, fresh reds with red fruit, violets and a mineral touch; Godello makes textured whites.

We have 15 Bierzo wines (red, white) from £11.50 to £92.50 a bottle, from Castilla y León. Mix them with any other wine in our shop.

Prices are per bottle when you buy 6 of the same wine (1–2 bottles +5%, 3–5 bottles +3%). All prices include UK duty and VAT. Free UK delivery on orders of £150 or more; otherwise £10.80 per box of 6.
