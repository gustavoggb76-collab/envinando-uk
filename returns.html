Returns, cancellations and refunds

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Your right to change your mind

If you are a consumer, you may cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of the day you receive the goods. To cancel, email hello@envinando.co.uk or message us on WhatsApp with your order details. You must then return the goods, unopened and in their original condition, within 14 days of telling us. You pay the cost of returning them unless they were faulty or not what you ordered.

Refunds

We refund the price of the goods and the standard delivery charge within 14 days of receiving the goods back (or proof that you have sent them), using the same payment method you used. We may reduce the refund if the goods have been opened, used or damaged beyond what is needed to inspect them.

When the right to cancel does not apply

By law, the 14-day right to cancel does not apply to:

  • wines sold en primeur or in bond, where the price was agreed at the time of sale, delivery takes place more than 30 days later, and the value depends on market fluctuations outside our control;
  • goods made to your specifications or clearly personalised (for example, engraved bottles or bespoke mixed cases).

Faulty or incorrect goods

If a wine is faulty (for example, corked or oxidised), damaged in transit or not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery. Please keep the bottle with at least two-thirds of the wine and the cork. We will collect it free of charge and give you a replacement or full refund. This does not affect your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Cancellation by us

If we cannot fulfil your order, we will tell you and refund you in full.
