La Mancha wines delivered in the UK

The largest vineyard area in Europe, on the high central plateau. La Mancha offers excellent value: juicy Tempranillo reds and fresh Airén, Verdejo and Sauvignon Blanc whites.

We have 26 La Mancha wines (red, rosé, rosé semi-sweet, sparkling, sparkling rosé, white, white semi-sweet) from £10.50 to £29.50 a bottle, from Castilla-La Mancha. Mix them with any other wine in our shop.

Prices are per bottle when you buy 6 of the same wine (1–2 bottles +5%, 3–5 bottles +3%). All prices include UK duty and VAT. Free UK delivery on orders of £150 or more; otherwise £10.80 per box of 6.
