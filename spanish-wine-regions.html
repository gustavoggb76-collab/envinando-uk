Spanish wine regions

Spain has more vineyard land than any other country, and every region has its own grapes, climate and style. Explore our wines region by region.

Ribera del Duero

Castilla y León · 76 wines from £9.24

High on the plateau along the Duero river, Ribera del Duero makes some of Spain's most celebrated reds from Tempranillo, known locally as Tinto Fino. Hot days and cold nights at 750–1,000 metres give deep colour, ripe black fruit and firm, fresh structure.

Castilla · La Mancha VT

Castilla-La Mancha · 34 wines from £8.65

Vino de la Tierra de Castilla covers Castilla-La Mancha and gives winemakers freedom to work with Spanish and international grapes. Expect modern, fruit-forward wines and great value.

Rioja

La Rioja, País Vasco and Navarra · 27 wines from £12.50

Spain's best-known wine region, on the upper Ebro. Rioja reds are built on Tempranillo, often with Garnacha, Graciano and Mazuelo, and are labelled by ageing — Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva — from fresh and fruity to mellow and complex.

La Mancha

Castilla-La Mancha · 26 wines from £10.50

The largest vineyard area in Europe, on the high central plateau. La Mancha offers excellent value: juicy Tempranillo reds and fresh Airén, Verdejo and Sauvignon Blanc whites.

Bierzo

Castilla y León · 15 wines from £11.50

A green, mountainous corner of north-west León where the Mencía grape gives fragrant, fresh reds with red fruit, violets and a mineral touch; Godello makes textured whites.

Rueda

Castilla y León · 13 wines from £8.20

Rueda is Spain's white-wine heartland. Verdejo is the star grape, giving crisp, aromatic whites with citrus, fennel and a gently bitter finish; Sauvignon Blanc also does well here.

Toro

Castilla y León · 13 wines from £11.50

Downstream from Ribera on the Duero, Toro is hot and dry. Its Tinta de Toro, a local Tempranillo, gives bold, ripe, full-bodied reds with plenty of power.

Castilla

Castilla-La Mancha · 12 wines from £10.50

Wines labelled Vino de la Tierra de Castilla come from across Castilla-La Mancha, with the freedom to blend Spanish and international grapes in a modern, fruit-forward style.

Jumilla

Murcia and Castilla-La Mancha · 12 wines from £10.95

A warm, dry region in the south-east where Monastrell rules, often from old bush vines. The reds are rich, ripe and generous, with dark fruit and soft spice.

Valdepeñas

Castilla-La Mancha · 11 wines from £10.50

An enclave in the south of La Mancha with a long tradition of smooth, oak-aged Tempranillo (Cencibel) reds at very friendly prices.

Ribera del Guadiana

Extremadura · 10 wines from £9.50

The DO of Extremadura, in south-west Spain near Portugal, producing ripe, generous reds and fresh whites at very good prices.

Rías Baixas

Galicia · 7 wines from £11.20

On Spain's green Atlantic coast, Rías Baixas is the home of Albariño: fresh, aromatic whites with citrus, stone fruit and a saline edge — a natural match for seafood.

Campo de Borja

Aragón · 7 wines from £12.50

Known as the 'Empire of Garnacha', Campo de Borja in Aragón makes juicy, ripe, generous Garnacha reds that are hard to beat for value.

Pago Abadía Retuerta

Castilla y León · 6 wines from £34.50

A single-estate Vino de Pago, the highest tier of Spanish wine classification, around the 12th-century abbey of Santa María de Retuerta on the Duero, next to Ribera del Duero.

Pago Valdepusa

Castilla-La Mancha · 6 wines from £18.40

Dominio de Valdepusa, in Toledo, was Spain's first Vino de Pago, the single-estate tier pioneered by Carlos Falcó, Marqués de Griñón.

Extremadura

Extremadura · 5 wines from £9.50

Wines from Extremadura in south-west Spain, a warm region near the Portuguese border making ripe, approachable reds and whites.

Almansa

Castilla-La Mancha · 4 wines from £10.50

In the east of Castilla-La Mancha, Almansa is known for Garnacha Tintorera, a rare red-fleshed grape, and Monastrell, giving dark, generous reds.

Castilla y León

Castilla y León · 4 wines from £10.95

Vino de la Tierra de Castilla y León covers the vast northern plateau around the Duero, home to modern, characterful wines made outside the region's DOs.

Pago Aylés

Aragón · 4 wines from £11.50

A single-estate Vino de Pago in Aragón, south of Zaragoza, making characterful wines from one historic property.

Vinos de Madrid

Madrid · 3 wines from £25.55

Vineyards in the hills around the capital, especially in the Sierra de Gredos, where old-vine Garnacha and Albillo are making some of Spain's most exciting wines.

Navarra

Navarra · 3 wines from £11.50

North of Rioja, Navarra is famous for its rosados and makes polished reds from Tempranillo, Garnacha and international varieties.

Somontano

Aragón · 3 wines from £12.50

At the foot of the Pyrenees, Somontano mixes Spanish and international grapes to make elegant, modern reds, whites and rosés.

Pago Campo de la Guardia

Castilla-La Mancha · 3 wines from £12.50

A single-estate Vino de Pago in La Guardia, Toledo, on the high plateau of Castilla-La Mancha.

Ribeiro

Galicia · 2 wines from £13.70

One of Galicia's historic regions, inland along the Miño, making fresh, aromatic whites from Treixadura, Godello and Albariño.

Calatayud

Aragón · 2 wines from £12.60

High, stony vineyards in Aragón with many old Garnacha vines, giving intense yet fresh reds.

Jerez

Andalucía · 2 wines from £39.55

The home of sherry, in the south-west corner of Andalucía, where sun-dried Pedro Ximénez grapes make some of the world's richest sweet wines.

Murcia IGP

Murcia · 2 wines from £15.50

Wines from the Murcia region of south-east Spain, where sunshine and Monastrell give ripe, warm, easy-drinking reds.

Priorat

Catalonia · 1 wines from £25.50

A rugged region of steep terraces and llicorella slate in Tarragona. Old Garnacha and Cariñena vines give powerful, mineral reds with great concentration; Priorat is one of only two Spanish regions with DOCa status.

Ribeira Sacra

Galicia · 1 wines from £22.90

Vineyards on dramatic terraces above the Miño and Sil rivers. Mencía here makes light, elegant, aromatic reds of real character.

Pago Florentino

Castilla-La Mancha · 1 wines from £15.50

A single-estate Vino de Pago in Malagón, Ciudad Real, among volcanic soils in Castilla-La Mancha.
