Rioja wines delivered in the UK

Spain's best-known wine region, on the upper Ebro. Rioja reds are built on Tempranillo, often with Garnacha, Graciano and Mazuelo, and are labelled by ageing — Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva — from fresh and fruity to mellow and complex.

We have 27 Rioja wines (red, sweet red) from £12.50 to £545.00 a bottle, from La Rioja, País Vasco and Navarra. Mix them with any other wine in our shop.

Prices are per bottle when you buy 6 of the same wine (1–2 bottles +5%, 3–5 bottles +3%). All prices include UK duty and VAT. Free UK delivery on orders of £150 or more; otherwise £10.80 per box of 6.
