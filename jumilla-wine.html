Jumilla wines delivered in the UK

A warm, dry region in the south-east where Monastrell rules, often from old bush vines. The reds are rich, ripe and generous, with dark fruit and soft spice.

We have 12 Jumilla wines (red) from £10.95 to £139.00 a bottle, from Murcia and Castilla-La Mancha. Mix them with any other wine in our shop.

Prices are per bottle when you buy 6 of the same wine (1–2 bottles +5%, 3–5 bottles +3%). All prices include UK duty and VAT. Free UK delivery on orders of £150 or more; otherwise £10.80 per box of 6.
