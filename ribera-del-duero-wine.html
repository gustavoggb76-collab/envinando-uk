Ribera del Duero wines delivered in the UK

High on the plateau along the Duero river, Ribera del Duero makes some of Spain's most celebrated reds from Tempranillo, known locally as Tinto Fino. Hot days and cold nights at 750–1,000 metres give deep colour, ripe black fruit and firm, fresh structure.

We have 76 Ribera del Duero wines (red, white) from £9.24 to £305.00 a bottle, from Castilla y León. Mix them with any other wine in our shop.

Prices are per bottle when you buy 6 of the same wine (1–2 bottles +5%, 3–5 bottles +3%). All prices include UK duty and VAT. Free UK delivery on orders of £150 or more; otherwise £10.80 per box of 6.
