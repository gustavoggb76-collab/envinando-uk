Pago Valdepusa wines delivered in the UK

Dominio de Valdepusa, in Toledo, was Spain's first Vino de Pago, the single-estate tier pioneered by Carlos Falcó, Marqués de Griñón.

We have 6 Pago Valdepusa wines (red) from £18.40 to £167.95 a bottle, from Castilla-La Mancha. Mix them with any other wine in our shop.

Prices are per bottle when you buy 6 of the same wine (1–2 bottles +5%, 3–5 bottles +3%). All prices include UK duty and VAT. Free UK delivery on orders of £150 or more; otherwise £10.80 per box of 6.
