Rueda wines delivered in the UK

Rueda is Spain's white-wine heartland. Verdejo is the star grape, giving crisp, aromatic whites with citrus, fennel and a gently bitter finish; Sauvignon Blanc also does well here.

We have 13 Rueda wines (white) from £8.20 to £31.95 a bottle, from Castilla y León. Mix them with any other wine in our shop.

Prices are per bottle when you buy 6 of the same wine (1–2 bottles +5%, 3–5 bottles +3%). All prices include UK duty and VAT. Free UK delivery on orders of £150 or more; otherwise £10.80 per box of 6.
