Almansa wines delivered in the UK

In the east of Castilla-La Mancha, Almansa is known for Garnacha Tintorera, a rare red-fleshed grape, and Monastrell, giving dark, generous reds.

We have 4 Almansa wines (red) from £10.50 to £24.70 a bottle, from Castilla-La Mancha. Mix them with any other wine in our shop.

Prices are per bottle when you buy 6 of the same wine (1–2 bottles +5%, 3–5 bottles +3%). All prices include UK duty and VAT. Free UK delivery on orders of £150 or more; otherwise £10.80 per box of 6.
