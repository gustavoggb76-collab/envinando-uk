Delivery

Last updated: 24 November 2026

  • Where we deliver: addresses in the United Kingdom. For the Scottish Highlands and Islands, Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man, please contact us before ordering so we can confirm availability and cost.
  • Cost: free on orders of £150 or more; below that, £10.80 per box of 6 bottles. The delivery charge is always shown in your basket before you pay.
  • When: we confirm the expected dispatch date when we confirm your order. Deliveries are made by courier on a tracked service, and you will receive tracking details by email or WhatsApp.
  • Age check on delivery: the recipient must be 18 or over and may be asked for photo ID. If nobody 18+ is available, the parcel will not be left and the courier will arrange redelivery or return it to us.
  • Failed deliveries: if an order is returned to us because no one was available or the address was incorrect, we may charge the cost of a second delivery, or refund the goods less the original delivery charge.
  • Damage on arrival: please check your parcel on delivery. If a bottle is broken or leaking, tell us within 48 hours at hello@envinando.co.uk with a photo and we will replace or refund it.
